03 — About

Behind the camera

I’m Khrystyna Donik, a photographer based in the Niagara Region. I shoot two worlds and refuse to pick a favourite: the businesses people pour their mornings into, and the families those mornings are actually for.

My sessions are unhurried on purpose. I’d rather wait twenty minutes for the light to be right than hand you forty frames of almost. People tell me they don’t photograph well; almost always, they just haven’t been photographed patiently.

Every image is edited by hand — no filters dumped on batches, no outsourced editing. What you receive looks like the day actually looked, only the way you’ll want to remember it.

How I shoot

  • Direction without stiffness — gentle prompts, never a pose you have to hold
  • The candid middle — the frames between the “ready” ones are usually the keepers
  • Every frame edited by hand, at full resolution, ready for print

06 — FAQ

The questions every client asks

Answers in plain English. If yours is not here, write to me — I reply to everything myself.

Do I own the photos, and can I use them anywhere?

You get full usage rights to every edited image: your website, social media, print, albums, flyers, directories and paid ads — no time limit, no extra licence fee. Most photographers charge commercial use separately; here it is included. I only keep the right to show a few images in this portfolio, and you can say no to even that.

Do I get the RAW files?

No. RAW files are the unedited camera originals, and not delivering them is standard practice in professional photography. You receive fully edited high-resolution images plus web-sized and vertical versions. Extra images edited after delivery are quoted per image, not sold as unedited files.

What if it rains, or the baby is fed the whole time?

Then we wait, or we move. Outdoor sessions shift to another day at no charge or go indoors, whichever you prefer, and newborn sessions follow the baby’s pace — feedings and soothing are part of the plan, not a problem. Rescheduling is free with 24 hours notice; inside 24 hours the session moves once at no charge.

When will we see the photos?

A small preview lands within 48 hours of every session, so you are never waiting in silence. Full galleries: within the week for The Mini, about two weeks for family sessions, 3 to 10 business days for business sessions depending on their length. Your gallery stays online for 12 months with unlimited downloads.

What should we wear, and how do we prepare?

Comfortable clothes you’d wear on a good day, in colours that don’t fight each other; skip big logos and neon. I’ll send a short styling note when you book, and we talk locations on the planning call. Kids need nothing but sleep and a snack — a toddler running around is not a problem, it’s usually the best material.

How far ahead should we book?

Two to four weeks is comfortable for family sessions; golden hour weekends in autumn go earlier. Newborn sessions are booked for your due window and simply flex to the actual arrival — the baby decides, the date moves, nothing is lost. A $75 retainer locks the date; the rest is due on the day.

Is there a travel fee?

None anywhere in the Niagara Region — St. Catharines, Niagara Falls, Welland, Niagara-on-the-Lake, Thorold, Grimsby and Fort Erie are all included. Further out in Southern Ontario, including Hamilton, Burlington and the GTA, is a flat $75 CAD agreed before you book.

What is not included in a session?

Video and drone work, multi-day shoots, weddings and large event coverage are quoted separately — I will say so plainly when that is what you need. Albums, framed prints and wall art are ordered at cost after delivery, never pushed during a session. Extra retouching beyond the delivered set is quoted per image.
